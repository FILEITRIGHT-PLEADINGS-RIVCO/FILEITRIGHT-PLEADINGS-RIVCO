Pro Per Pleading Builder
A free tool for people representing themselves in the Superior Court of California, County of Riverside.
Fill in the blanks, and the builder lays your words onto 28-line California pleading paper and gives you a text-searchable PDF.
What it does
Puts your name and contact information on page 1, starting at line 1, and leaves the clerk's stamp space blank (Cal. Rules of Court, rule 2.111)
Places the court's name on line 8, the case title on the left, and the case number and document title on the right
Uses 12-point Times, double-spaced lines numbered 1 to 28, and correct margins (rules 2.104, 2.107, 2.108)
Numbers every page and adds the document title in the footer (rules 2.109, 2.110)
Adds a statement under penalty of perjury for declarations (Code Civ. Proc., § 2015.5)
Counts pages so you can track the Rule 3.1113 limits for memoranda
Makes a text-searchable PDF, which Riverside requires for e-filing
Privacy
Everything happens on your own phone or computer. Nothing you type is sent anywhere. Your draft is saved only in your browser.
Install it like an app
Open the site on your phone, then use your browser's menu and choose Add to Home Screen. After the first visit it works without internet.
Important
This tool formats your papers. It does not give legal advice or check what your document says. Before filing, confirm your case number, hearing details, and deadlines. If you are unsure which form or paper to file, ask the court's self-help center.
Made by a Riverside pro per, for Riverside pro pers. Free for anyone representing themselves.
